# Elon Musk 3D avatar shortlist and qualification plan

Research date: **2026-11-11, Asia/Seoul**. Target: the recognizable appearance and conversational behavior of **Musk in 2026**, as defined in the [persona roadmap](../2026-persona-roadmap.md).

## Decision

Existing Musk-specific 3D assets with advertised facial rigs and blendshapes are available. Evaluate those assets before investing further in the current generic anatomical proxy or choosing a video-only interface. **Chatiz Characters and star-game-3d-media are the first technical inspection candidates**; Meta Avatars is a useful head-focused alternative.

This ranking is based on declared controls, editable formats and likely integration effort. It is **not a measured visual-quality ranking**. No model was purchased, downloaded, imported or animated during this research. No candidate has been verified as a 2026 likeness, as compatible with this application, or as ready for deployment.

## Evidence rules

- Product specifications below are seller/listing claims. A convincing preview render does not prove the included mesh, rig or browser result.
- Prices are values displayed in retrieved pages on the research date; some pages were indexed earlier. Recheck the selected SKU, currency, region, tax and terms before acquisition.
- USD is written only where the listing explicitly exposed it. A bare dollar sign preserves the source's display without resolving its currency code.
- "Rigged" can mean body bones only. "Facial rig" does not guarantee blendshapes, native visemes or a particular control vocabulary.
- "Ready for rigging" means rigging work is still required. A fixed speaking animation does not imply lip-sync to new speech.
- Sketchfab metadata was available through indexed publisher listings, while direct page access returned HTTP 403 in this research. Actual downloadable packages and their current contents remain uninspected.

## 4. Distribution and acquisition record

Keep asset selection separate from the open-source application license. The [ArtStation EULA](https://www.artstation.com/marketplace-product-eula), [CGTrader Royalty Free guidance](https://help.cgtrader.com/hc/en-us/articles/360015124437-Royalty-Free-License) and [Sketchfab Standard terms](https://sketchfab.com/licenses) restrict redistribution of source assets in ways that differ from permission to use them in a finished work. A purchased FBX or an openly downloadable browser GLB must not automatically be committed to this repository.

ArtStation's [buyer FAQ](https://help.artstation.com/en/articles/16155217-general-faq-for-buyers) says the selected EULA takes precedence over contrary product-description restrictions. Record the conflicting Chatiz wording and the actual agreement instead of silently resolving it from a price tier. The listed TurboSquid editorial restriction also remains explicit.

For a candidate selected later, record creator, exact URL/SKU, acquisition date, receipt/license text, version/hash, derivative changes, attribution and permitted distribution path. A marketplace label does not independently verify authorship, a real scan or all rights associated with a depicted person.

Prefer one of these documented delivery choices, according to the actual terms: an eligible redistributable asset bundled with attribution; a user-supplied local asset; or separately licensed hosting/rendering. A private source file is not a blanket solution for every use restriction. Do not contact sellers or purchase a model as a side effect of this document.

## 5. Audition procedure for the application

The current [humanoid runtime](../humanoid-avatar.md) exposes 52 face channels and 15 visemes, uses calibrated rig mapping and supports local GLB import. Those capabilities can support a new face, but external rigs require their own mapping and inspection.

### Step 1 — Establish the comparison

Use dated 2026 appearance references and the same camera, lighting, speech and browser conditions for every candidate. Keep front, three-quarter and profile views. Score neutral likeness separately from likeness while talking. Preserve original seller files and create a versioned conversion output.

### Step 2 — Inspect the actual file

Inventory mesh counts, topology, skin weights, bones, morph names, nonzero morph displacement, animation tracks, units, scale and rest pose. Verify skin, eyes, eyelashes, hair, clothing, teeth, tongue and mouth interior. Test gaze and independent eyelid closure; do not infer hidden structures from a thumbnail.

### Step 3 — Map facial articulation

Map supported facial bones/morphs to the runtime controls. Record missing controls explicitly and choose whether to author them, approximate them or reject the candidate. Test bilabial closure, lip rounding, wide vowels and jaw opening. Keep coarticulation smooth, and prevent native visemes, expression morphs and jaw bones from applying duplicate movement.

### Step 4 — Convert and inspect in the browser

Export the appropriate Blender/FBX source to GLB with skinning, facial targets and intended animation intact. Check texture color spaces, hair transparency, normals and material appearance in the actual application. Reallusion/Maya/Arnold shaders are not automatically portable to browser materials. Create LODs or texture reductions only after a visible comparison; record what changed.

### Step 5 — Exercise natural conversation

Run fixed evaluation audio covering short and longer answers, pauses and emphasis, then an unseen generated utterance. Check silence-to-speech transitions, eye contact, blinks, head continuity and interruption. A prerecorded loop must not force unrelated smiles or gestures. English and Korean speech need separate articulation checks.

### Step 6 — Measure and choose

Measure asset load time, memory, frame time and interaction under simultaneous inference/audio load. Collect human comparisons for facial likeness, mouth/teeth artifacts, gaze and overall naturalness. Preserve failures. Selection requires a usable acquisition/distribution path, verified facial control, acceptable target-device performance and an observed improvement over the proxy; a seller's realism claim alone cannot pass.

## 6. Required qualification report

| Report section | Required output |
| --- | --- |
| Provenance and version | Creator, item/SKU, hashes, receipt/terms, source and conversion files |
| 2026 appearance | Dated reference set, front/three-quarter/profile comparisons, remaining mismatch |
| Control mapping | Actual bones/morphs, supported 52-channel/15-viseme mapping, gaps and authored fixes |
| Materials and conversion | Source-to-GLB differences, missing maps, hair/eye handling, LOD decisions |
| Dynamic evidence | Same-audio clips, unseen speech, blinks/gaze, interruptions and silence transitions |
| Performance | Machine/browser, concurrent services, load/memory/frame metrics |
| Review | Named evaluation method, human/model-assisted distinction, scores and unresolved issues |
| Decision | Accept, adapt or reject, with reason and permitted delivery method |

The next visual implementation should produce this report for a small set of actual assets. The final decision remains open until files and generated speech have been evaluated together.

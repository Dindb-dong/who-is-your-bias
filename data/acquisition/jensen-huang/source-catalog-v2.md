## Local storage

The catalog stores URLs and source locators. This collection acquired no
video or audio files. Full transcripts stay at their publisher links. If a
later task requires media for verification, estimate bytes from duration and
bitrate and use a small working set of needed clips. At 30 hours of media,
2–5 Mbps video would occupy about 27–67.5 GB (decimal). An audio-only copy
at 64 kbps would occupy about 0.86 GB for the same duration, as a comparison
of storage costs. Text-only research can proceed with the available transcripts.
